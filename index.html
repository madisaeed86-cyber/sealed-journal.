Private by design, pretty by default

Sealed Journal is a calm place to write. It keeps your words on your own device and encrypts them with your PIN before they are saved.

Locked with your PIN

Choose a 4 or 6-digit PIN. Pages are encrypted with it, and the journal locks itself when you step away.

Fonts, moods and stickers

Checklists too

30 journal prompts for when the page feels empty

Borrow one of these whenever you don't know what to write. There's no wrong answer.

For slow mornings

  • What do I want today to feel like?
  • One small thing I'm looking forward to.
  • What's the first sound I heard this morning?
  • Something I can let go of before noon.
  • A kind sentence to myself, written like a note from a friend.

For the end of the day

  • What felt soft today?
  • Three moments I want to remember.
  • Where did my attention keep drifting?
  • What drained me, and what filled me back up?
  • What am I carrying into tomorrow?

For hard days

  • What exactly is heavy right now? Name it plainly.
  • What would I tell a friend who felt this way?
  • One thing that is still okay.
  • What do I need tonight: rest, company, or quiet?
  • Something I got through before that felt this big.

For knowing yourself

  • A place where I feel most like myself.
  • Something I believed a year ago that I don't anymore.
  • What do I keep saying yes to that I'd like to say no to?
  • The people who make me feel lighter.
  • A habit I'd like to keep for the next ten years.

For noticing small joys

  • Describe the light in the room right now.
  • The best thing I ate this week.
  • A song that matched today's mood.
  • Something that made me laugh without trying.
  • A tiny thing someone did that I appreciated.

For looking ahead

  • A letter to myself one year from now.
  • What would make next week feel good?
  • One skill I'd love to be learning.
  • A place I want to visit, and who I'd take.
  • What does a really ordinary, happy day look like for me?

Questions

Can anyone else read my journal?

No. Your pages are encrypted with a key made from your PIN and stored only on your device. We have no server that receives your writing, so we can't read it either.

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Because your pages are encrypted with it, a forgotten PIN can't be recovered. You can erase the journal and start again, or restore an older backup if you remember that backup's PIN.

Will my pages be there on my other phone?

Each device keeps its own journal. To move pages, download a backup in Settings and restore it on the other device.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, after you've opened it once. Adding it to your home screen makes it open like a normal app.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every theme, font, sticker and feature is included. There's nothing to buy.
