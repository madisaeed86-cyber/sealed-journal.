The short version

Your journal pages and checklists are encrypted and stay on your device. We never receive them. There are no accounts and nothing to buy.

Your journal

Your pages, checklists, moods, fonts and stickers are stored in your browser's storage on your own device. They are encrypted with a key made from your PIN before they are saved. Your PIN itself is never stored. We cannot read, recover or reset your journal, and we cannot recover a forgotten PIN.

Advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense on the landing page and on the journal's home and checklist screens. Ads are never shown on the page you write in, and advertisers never receive your journal content. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, which may include personalised ads. You can manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Things stored on your device

Besides your encrypted journal, the app stores your theme choice, your last-used font and the date of your last backup. These stay on your device.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which lets Google see your IP address. The website host ([HOSTING PROVIDER]) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and request times.

Your PIN and backups

You are responsible for remembering your PIN. Because your pages are encrypted with it, we cannot recover them if you forget it or if your browser data is cleared. Download a backup from Settings now and then to keep your pages safe.

Backup files

If you use "Download backup", an encrypted file is saved to your device. Where you keep that file is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use "Erase journal" on the lock screen, or clear this website's data in your browser settings. Both remove everything from your device.

Children

Sealed Journal is not directed at children under 13.
